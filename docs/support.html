Support & FAQ

Pong Pal Showdown is a small, single-developer hobby project. The fastest way to reach me is email — I read every message, but please be patient on response time.

How do I play multiplayer?

  1. Make sure both Apple Watches are on the same Wi-Fi network and both running watchOS 10 or later.
  2. On both watches, open Pong Pal Showdown and tap Multiplayer.
  3. Wait until each watch sees the other in the "Nearby Players" list.
  4. One player taps the other player's name to send an invite. The other player taps Accept.
  5. The inviting player picks a target score: First to 3, First to 5, or No limit.
  6. The match begins after a 3-2-1 countdown.

My friend's watch doesn't appear in the list. What's wrong?

  • Confirm both watches are on the same Wi-Fi network — some routers create separate networks for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, or for "guest" networks.
  • The first time you use multiplayer, watchOS asks permission to access the local network. If you tapped Don't Allow, multiplayer won't work. Re-enable it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  • Both watches must be running watchOS 10 or later.
  • Public Wi-Fi networks (coffee shops, airports) often block peer-to-peer connections. Try a home or trusted Wi-Fi network.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Pong Pal Showdown is a standalone Apple Watch app — there is no iPhone companion required, and no iPhone version available.

The Digital Crown isn't moving my paddle.

  • Make sure the watch face is the active focus (tap the screen once to ensure focus is on the game, not the system).
  • If you're playing in the simulator on Mac, click the simulator window first, then use the left/right arrow keys.

How do I report a bug?

Email me with:

  • What watch model you have (e.g., Series 9 / Ultra 2)
  • What watchOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • What you were doing when it happened
  • A screen recording if you can capture one
