Pong With Friends

Play Pong head-to-head on two Apple Watches.

Pong With Friends is a watchOS game that lets you play classic Pong against another Apple Watch over local Wi-Fi. No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection.

Available on the Apple Watch App Store for $0.99.
